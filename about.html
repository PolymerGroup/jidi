About / Jinan, Shandong

A peptide supplier built around identity, not inventory.

Shandong Jidi Biotechnology Co., Ltd. supplies research peptides, sequence-defined custom synthesis and bulk research material to laboratories, CROs and qualified distributors. The catalog is designed for technical buyers: every entity has a CAS or sequence reference, a pack matrix and a documented analytical path.

Shandong Jidi Biotechnology Co., Ltd. Jidi Bio / Jidi Peptides Jinan · Shandong · China Global dispatch

What we do

We supply catalog research peptides in 10-vial packs and quote custom sequences, modifications and larger-scale material. The commercial model is intentionally simple: technical inquiry, written specification, documented lot, tracked dispatch.

Jidi Bio is the brand under which Shandong Jidi Biotechnology Co., Ltd. sells research reagents. Jidi Peptides is the catalog identity used for peptide-specific product pages, documentation and distributor communication.

Capabilities

  • Catalog peptides across metabolic, repair, cosmetic, mitochondrial, endocrine, neuroscience, reproductive and blend categories.
  • Custom synthesis from 5 mg feasibility batches to gram and kilogram programs.
  • RP-HPLC, ESI-MS, Karl Fischer, counter-ion and optional project-specific analytical testing.
  • Lyophilized powder, liquid fill, pre-filled vials and 10-vial research kits.
  • Private-label documentation and mixed-pack support for qualified distributors.

Compliance boundary

Research use is the boundary, not a disclaimer at the bottom.

Jidi Bio supplies materials for laboratory research and manufacturing use. We do not position any product as a drug, food, cosmetic, dietary supplement or medical device, and we do not provide clinical, dosing or veterinary guidance. Buyers are responsible for confirming that their intended use, import and handling comply with applicable law and institutional policy.

DOCUMENTATION PRINCIPLES

01 · No invented CAS numbers. If a public CAS is not assigned, the record says so.

02 · No borrowed seals. Illustrative traces are labelled and never presented as a lot COA.

03 · No clinical claims. Mechanism text is research context, not a therapeutic claim.

04 · Lot-specific release. The COA belongs to the lot being quoted.
