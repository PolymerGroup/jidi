Research overview

A cell-permeable cardiolipin-binding tetrapeptide for mitochondrial membrane and oxidative-stress research.

Partitions into the inner mitochondrial membrane and binds cardiolipin, stabilizing respiratory-chain supercomplexes and reducing mitochondrial ROS in preclinical models.

Research context

Mitochondrial bioenergetics, cardiolipin biology, oxidative stress. This is a laboratory research reagent; the information is not a therapeutic claim and does not establish safety or efficacy in humans or animals.

How is SS-31 analytically verified?
