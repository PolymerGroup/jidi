    Contact FAQ
    Before you send
    Four answers that reduce back-and-forth on the first inquiry.
    
    
    
    
    
  What should I include in a first inquiry?
  
    Include the product name or CAS, pack size, quantity, destination country and any documentation requirement. For custom work, include the sequence, modifications, purity target, counter-ion and scale. A complete first message shortens the quote cycle.
    

    
  How quickly will I receive a reply?
  
    Inquiries are reviewed Monday–Friday, 09:00–18:00 CST (UTC+8), typically within one business day. Custom or bulk inquiries that need sequence review may take longer; the first reply will confirm what is needed.
    

    
  Can I request a COA before ordering?
  
    Yes. Send the catalog number and pack SKU for the lot being considered. We will confirm availability and provide the lot-specific COA so your receiving and analytical teams can review it before the purchase order.
    

    
  Do you ship internationally?
  
    We dispatch internationally and confirm shipping mode, packing and documentation at quote. The buyer is responsible for import permits, customs clearance and compliance with local regulations for research materials.
    

    
  What payment terms are available?
  
    Orders are confirmed by proforma invoice and are typically paid by T/T or a major transfer service. Qualified accounts may be considered for net terms after review. The proforma states the currency, bank charges and any documentation conditions before dispatch.
