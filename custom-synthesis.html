Custom synthesis FAQ

What is the minimum quantity for custom peptide synthesis?

Feasibility batches can start at 5 mg for short sequences. A typical custom quote is 20–50 mg at ≥95% purity, with scale-up from 100 mg to grams and kilograms. The exact minimum depends on sequence length, hydrophobicity, modifications and purification difficulty.

How long does custom synthesis take?

Simple unmodified peptides typically quote 2–4 weeks after sequence and documentation approval. Long, hydrophobic, disulfide-rich or heavily modified sequences can require 4–6 weeks or a staged feasibility study. The written quote states the expected lead time and any analytical milestones.

What purity grades and salt forms are available?

RP-HPLC purity grades from crude through ≥70%, ≥80%, ≥90%, ≥95%, ≥98% and ≥99% are quoted according to sequence difficulty. TFA, acetate and HCl salt forms are available where chemically appropriate; counter-ion and residual TFA are stated in the COA.

Can you handle long sequences and complex modifications?

Sequences from 2 to 120 amino acids are routinely reviewed. Longer constructs, multiple disulfides, difficult hydrophobic stretches, non-natural amino acids and conjugation chemistry are assessed case by case before a quote is issued.

Do you support bulk API and distributor requirements?

Yes. We quote research-grade bulk material, large-pack fill, mixed catalog packs, private-label documentation and scheduled repeat supply. We do not claim cGMP manufacturing or a regulatory approval; documentation needs should be stated at inquiry so the scope can be evaluated explicitly.

What documentation is included with a custom lot?

The standard package includes a lot-specific COA, RP-HPLC chromatogram and integration result, ESI-MS identity data, sequence or composition reference, appearance, net content, counter-ion where applicable, storage and retest date. Additional method development or third-party testing can be quoted separately.

What payment terms apply to custom and bulk programs?

Custom and bulk projects are confirmed by proforma invoice. Payment is typically by T/T or a major transfer service; milestone or net terms may be offered to qualified accounts after commercial review. The schedule, currency and documentation requirements are fixed in the proforma.
